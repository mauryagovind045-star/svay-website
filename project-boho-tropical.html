Boho Tropical Villa

Layered boho-tropical textures for a villa in Assagao — cane, greenery and ease.

Location
Assagao, Goa
Completed
2021
Type
Private villa
Scope
Interiors & styling

One of the studio's earliest villa stories, this Assagao home embraces Goa's easy rhythm: cane and rattan furniture, layered natural textures, and greenery woven through every room.

The result is a villa that feels collected rather than decorated — relaxed, tactile and unmistakably tropical. Completed in 2021.
