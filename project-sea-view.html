Sea View 3BHK Apartment

A three-bedroom apartment opened up to light, breeze and the sea beyond.

Location

Completed

Type

Scope
Interior design & styling

The brief for this Porvorim apartment was simple: let the view lead. The design keeps sightlines long and surfaces soft, so daylight and the sea breeze move freely through the three bedrooms and shared spaces.

A muted, sun-washed palette, gentle curves and unfussy detailing keep attention where it belongs — on the horizon. Completed in 2024.
