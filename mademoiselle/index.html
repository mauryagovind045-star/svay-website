Mademoiselle — a boutique hotel

Gilt, velvet and deep-glazed tile — a boutique hotel where every room keeps its own character.

Interiors & styling

Mademoiselle is a hotel that refuses to repeat itself. Behind a pale facade of stacked arched balconies, each room is given its own temperament — a rust headboard here, a scalloped grey one there, an arched black four-poster in the next — so that no two stays read quite the same.

The constants are the details. Gilt mirrors catch the light in every room; velvet armchairs in burgundy, mint and cream sit beside slim bobbin tables; brass sconces and single pendants do the work of overhead lighting. The bathrooms are where the studio lets go: floor-to-ceiling glazed tile in navy and oxblood, arched brass mirrors, stone basins on black steel, and one suite given over to a freestanding bath set among planting.

It is hospitality detailed like a home — generous with colour and texture, and built so that each guest remembers which room was theirs.
