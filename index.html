The design journey

Five stages, clearly signed off

  1. 01

    Foundation

    Concept & theme development · layout options · zoning & furniture plan · look-and-feel moodboard

    4 weeks
  2. 02

    Definition

    Material & finish selections · kitchen drawings · wall treatments · false-ceiling drawings

    2–3 weeks
  3. 03

    Dimension

    3D model · electrical drawings · plumbing layout

    3 weeks
  4. 04

    Detail

    Detailed furniture drawings · upholstery & curtains · paint selection · lighting list

    2–3 weeks
  5. 05

    Reveal

    Decor selection · compiled design document · staging, styling & photoshoot

    1 week

Clear sign-offs close each stage; the next begins on your approval.
